# Licence and attribution for the data in `data/real/`

This file covers **data only**. It does not license the SafarSet source code (see the README, "Data & licences").

## GTFS-derived files: ODbL

These files are extracts and flattenings of the community Chennai GTFS feed:

- `reference/bus_routes.csv`, `reference/bus_trips.csv`, `reference/bus_route_stops.csv`, `reference/bus_stops.csv`, `reference/bus_manifest.json`

Source: **UngalSoththu / ChennaiGTFS**, <https://github.com/ungalsoththu/ChennaiGTFS> (`data/chennai-unified-gtfs.zip`, MobilityDatabase mdb-3360).

**Attribution (required): "UngalSoththu / ChennaiGTFS".**

These files are a derivative database of that compilation. The ChennaiGTFS README states that the data compiled there is published under
"ODbL / PDDL", with attribution, and asks that derivatives be kept open. We take the stricter of the two: **the files listed above are
offered under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0**, <https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/>. Anyone who redistributes them,
or a database derived from them, must keep this attribution and offer their derivative under the same licence.

### What is and is not established

- The ChennaiGTFS repository is internally inconsistent: its `LICENSE` file is MIT, while its README gives the data licence as ODbL / PDDL.
  We follow the README's data licence and the project's request to keep derivatives open.
- **The underlying MTC timetable data was scraped by the ChennaiGTFS project from the MTC app, and no terms of use for it are stated.
  MTC's own terms are UNVERIFIED.** A licence from the compilers cannot grant rights that MTC may hold in its own data.
- The CMRL and Southern Railway source files in that project are (c) their agencies and come from a portal with no explicit licence.
  Only the MTC bus routes, stops and trips are used here; no CMRL or Southern Railway file is redistributed.
- Timings in the feed are templated per route, not observed (see `SOURCES.md`).

If MTC or another rights holder objects, remove the files above and regenerate them from a copy of the feed you are entitled to use
(`scripts/extract_bus_reference.py`).

## Station coordinates: OpenStreetMap

`reference/stations.json` holds station coordinates from OpenStreetMap (via Nominatim), not from the GTFS feed:
(c) OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the ODbL, <https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright>.

## Everything else in `data/`

`trains.json`, `capacities.json` and `data/simulated/` are hand-compiled from secondary public sources or generated by the project;
provenance and confidence are in `SOURCES.md`. Simulated data is labelled SIMULATED wherever it appears.
